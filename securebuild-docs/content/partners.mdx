# Partners: Secure Open Source Projects

SecureBuild partners with open source projects to provide continuously secured builds to the community. Our partner ecosystem ensures enterprise-grade security with direct upstream support.

## Partner Benefits

- **Automated Security**: Your project gets continuous CVE monitoring and automated secure rebuilds
- **Increased Adoption**: Make it easier for enterprises to adopt your project with compliance-ready images
- **Community Trust**: Demonstrate commitment to security with transparent vulnerability tracking
- **Zero Maintenance**: We handle the security scanning, patching, and rebuild infrastructure

## How to Partner

If you maintain an open source project and want to join our partner ecosystem:

1. **Visit the Catalog**: Browse to [securebuild.com/images](https://securebuild.com/images)
2. **Click "Partner With Us"**: Located in the top navigation
3. **Submit Your Project**: Tell us about your project and security goals
4. **Evaluation**: Our team reviews your project for partnership fit
5. **Integration**: We set up automated rebuilds and monitoring
6. **Launch**: Your secure builds become available in the catalog
