# Overview

The Dashboard Overview provides a comprehensive snapshot of your SecureBuild usage and activity.

## Image Usage

The **Image Usage** section displays daily download metrics across your subscribed images:

- **Visual Graph**: Interactive chart showing download activity over the past week
- **Daily Breakdown**: See which days had the most image pulls
- **Usage Trends**: Track your team's consumption patterns over time
- **Empty State**: When you first start, the graph will be empty until you begin pulling images

## Quick Actions

The **Quick Actions** panel provides shortcuts to common tasks:

- **Subscribe To A New Image**: Browse the catalog and add new images to your subscription
- **Invite Team Member**: Add team members to your organization for collaborative access
- **Manage Subscriptions**: View and modify your current image subscriptions and billing

## Recent Activity

The **Recent Activity** feed shows your latest account actions:

- **Image Pulls**: Track when images were downloaded and from which project
- **Timestamps**: See exactly when each action occurred
- **Project Context**: Understand which project or environment triggered each pull
- **Scrollable History**: Access older activity by scrolling through the feed

## Getting Started

1. **Review Usage**: Check your image usage patterns and trends
2. **Use Quick Actions**: Subscribe to images and invite team members
3. **Monitor Activity**: Stay informed about recent account activity
4. **Set Up Notifications**: Configure alerts for your subscribed images
